<objective>
Break up `src/lib/auto-sync.ts` (CODEDUP-02 / ARCH-REMEDIATION-PLAN Fase 5.3):
a 974-line god module mixing a scheduler/loop (`startAutoSync`, `checkAndSync`,
`recoverStuckSyncLogs`, `runStartupSync`), a cooldown policy (`getSefazCooldown`),
and 3 independent sync strategies (`syncViaSefaz`, `syncViaNsdocs`,
`syncViaReceitaNfse`) hardcoded with direct branching. Split into
`sync-scheduler.ts` (loop/scheduling/cooldown/recovery) and
`sync-strategies/{sefaz,nsdocs,receita-nfse}.ts`, each implementing a common
`SyncStrategy` contract the scheduler calls polymorphically.

Purpose: isolate the 3 sync integrations so a change to one (e.g. SEFAZ retry
logic) can't accidentally touch the others, and make the scheduler's dispatch
uniform instead of 3 copy-pasted trigger blocks.
Output: `sync-scheduler.ts` + `sync-strategies/` directory; `auto-sync.ts`
deleted; the 2 external consumers (`bootstrap.ts`, `nsdocs/sync/route.ts`)
repointed to the new modules with zero behavior change.
</objective>
